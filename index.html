Catalogue curation

Can a model read a messy product listing and put it on the right shelf? Five parts on 25,022 real Shopify products and 141 categories.

  • Cleaned a public catalogue, and found the answer hidden in one of its fields.
  • Rebuilt a neural network from 25.5% to 53.5%, past a logistic regression baseline.
  • LLMs with no training reached 73% on a 100-product check. Fine-tuning a small one is next.
